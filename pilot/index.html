Help test a lesson

Try one teaching pack and help us learn what works. The materials have automated checks, but their difficulty levels and teaching times have not yet been validated in classrooms.

This is a guide for collecting feedback, not a claim that a classroom study has been completed.

1. Choose one skill

Pick a teaching pack appropriate to your group. Record the skill number, a broad learner age band, group size, and whether learners have encountered the topic before. Do not record student names.

2. Try the planned sequence

Use the warm-up, main activity, discussion, and exit ticket. Record actual time for each part and any extra explanation you needed to give. Note which words or instructions caused hesitation before showing a model answer.

3. Check the reasoning

Use the pack's visible success checks. Count how many responses explain the relevant reasoning, how many give only an answer, and how many suggest a recurring misunderstanding. Record counts and brief anonymous patterns, not identifiable student work.

4. Try a different example

Use a fresh scenario for the same skill, or a focused practice question that was not shown in the lesson. Ask learners to explain why their answer follows. Record whether they can use the reasoning without copying the original model. One small group cannot establish a general improvement effect.

5. Share what to improve

Send classroom feedback: the skill or question ID, broad learner group, actual timings, the confusing instruction, and your suggested change. If a question has more than one defensible answer, explain both interpretations.
