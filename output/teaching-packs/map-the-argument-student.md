# See how an argument fits together

**XiXteen | Student handout | Skill 03: What's the Argument?**

Identify the reasons, conclusion, and background, then show what supports what.

Suggested teaching time: 20 minutes. All scenarios and figures are invented for reasoning practice.

Name: __________________________  Date: ______________

## Useful words

- **Conclusion:** The statement the argument gives reasons to accept.
- **Reason:** A statement offered in support of the conclusion.
- **Background:** Context that helps describe the situation without being offered as support.

## Warm-up: The closed gallery

Invented scenario: A visitor says, 'The gallery is closed every Monday. Today is Monday. Therefore, the gallery is closed today.'

**Produce:** Write the conclusion and the two reasons. Show how the reasons support the conclusion.

1. Copy the statement being supported.
2. Copy both supporting statements.
3. Draw arrows from the two reasons to the conclusion, or write how the reasons work together.

**Before you finish:** Do both reasons lead toward the statement about today, rather than away from it?

## Main activity: Compare two fundraisers

Invented scenario: A club member writes: 'Our club meets on Tuesdays. Last month's bake sale earned $300 after costs, while the car wash earned $180. Therefore, we should hold another bake sale next month.'

**Produce:** Label the three sentences, draw one support arrow, and write one explanatory sentence.

1. Label each sentence background, reason, or conclusion.
2. Draw an arrow from the reason to the conclusion, or write 'reason -> conclusion' using the actual sentences.
3. Explain in one sentence why the Tuesday meeting detail is not support for the fundraising recommendation.

**Before you finish:** Does your arrow show which statement supports which? The most detailed sentence need not be the conclusion.

## Exit ticket: The picnic plan

Invented scenario: A club member writes, 'Our club was founded in 2018. The forecast predicts heavy rain during tomorrow's picnic. We should move tomorrow's picnic indoors.'

**Produce:** Label the three sentences and show the support relation; explain one label.

1. Label each sentence background, reason, or conclusion.
2. Draw an arrow from the supporting sentence to the conclusion, or write the relation in words.
3. Explain why the founding year does not support the proposed move.

**Before you finish:** Have you identified what supports the recommendation without treating the weather forecast as a guarantee?
