# Keep the conclusion within the evidence

**XiXteen | Student handout | Skill 08: Does the Logic Follow?**

Notice when a conclusion reaches beyond the people or results actually studied.

Suggested teaching time: 20 minutes. All scenarios and figures are invented for reasoning practice.

Name: __________________________  Date: ______________

## Useful words

- **Conclusion:** The statement an argument asks us to accept on the basis of its reasons.
- **Sample:** The people or cases actually studied.
- **Representative sample:** A group selected to reflect the wider population relevant to the question.

## Warm-up: A club speaks for everyone?

Invented scenario: Nine of ten chess-club members asked say they want a lunchtime tournament. The club leader says, 'Every student in our school wants one.' No students outside the club were asked.

**Produce:** Write one sentence identifying the jump and one narrower conclusion.

1. Name the group asked and the group covered by the leader's conclusion.
2. Write a conclusion that keeps both the surveyed group and the nine-of-ten result.

**Before you finish:** Does your rewrite avoid both “all club members” and “all students”?

## Main activity: The grading-rubric survey

Invented scenario: Eighteen of 24 students in one art class say they prefer a new grading rubric. The other six say they do not prefer it. The principal announces, 'Every student in our 600-student school prefers it.' No other students were asked.

**Produce:** Write a scope comparison, a supported conclusion, and one further evidence need.

1. Complete: 'The evidence covers ___, but the conclusion covers ___.'
2. Rewrite the conclusion as one sentence that the survey actually supports.
3. Name one additional kind of evidence needed before estimating school-wide preferences.

**Before you finish:** Does your rewrite retain the art-class sample and the six students who did not prefer the rubric?

## Exit ticket: A survey of park volunteers

Invented scenario: A town has 2,000 residents. Twelve of 15 residents at a park clean-up say they favor Sunday street closures. The mayor says, 'Most town residents favor the closures.' No other residents were asked.

**Produce:** Write three sentences: the gap, a supported conclusion, and a better evidence plan.

1. Compare the residents actually asked with those covered by the mayor's conclusion.
2. Rewrite the result without extending it beyond those surveyed.
3. Describe who a new survey should include to estimate town-wide support.

**Before you finish:** Have you noticed that even a claim about “most” can reach beyond an unrepresentative sample?
