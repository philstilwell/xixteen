Classroom use & adaptation

You may use XiXteen's original teaching materials in your classroom and adapt them for your students, free of charge, with credit. Commercial resale is not permitted.

You may

  • Download and print student handouts, instructor guides, and reference materials for your classes.
  • Share the materials with your students, including through your class learning platform.
  • Adapt the wording, examples, level, format, or timing for your teaching.

Please give credit

Keep a credit to Phil Stilwell and XiXteen, with a link to xixteen.com. If you change a resource, identify it as an adaptation.

Suggested credit: “Adapted from XiXteen by Phil Stilwell — xixteen.com. Changes made by [your name].” For an unchanged resource, omit “Adapted from” and the changes note.

Limits

Do not sell or resell XiXteen materials or adaptations. This permission concerns XiXteen's original educational materials; it does not grant rights to third-party sources, quotations, or linked content. Those remain subject to their own terms.

For another use, request permission. These classroom terms do not provide a separate software licence for the site's code.
