Privacy & saved progress

You can practise without an account or email address. Public score sharing is optional and is off for a new profile.

Saved in this browser

The learner page saves your display name, a randomly generated participant identifier, completed practice statistics, and separate daily and practice sessions. A saved session includes its questions, answers, your place, elapsed active time, and any return link to an article.

This information uses browser storage on this device. It does not follow you to another browser or device. Clearing site data removes it; private browsing or blocked storage can prevent saving. The page shows a notice if saving fails.

Sessions resume automatically when you return. The daily session applies to its original date; practice can be resumed on a later day. Time spent away from the page or in another mode is paused.

Optional public scores

If you turn on “Share daily scores” before finishing a daily board, XiXteen sends your participant identifier, display name, quiz date, selected answers, and response times to its scoring service. The service checks the answers, stores the result and skill statistics, and may show your name, score, and completion time on public daily and weekly boards.

The scoring service also derives a hashed duplicate-check signal from the participant identifier and connection information. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes network information such as IP addresses to serve requests. Loading public boards and checking previously shared results also contacts the scoring service.

Turning sharing off prevents new submissions. It does not withdraw a score already shared. Practice sessions are not submitted to the public scoreboard.

Reset and shared devices

“Reset” clears this site's saved profile, sessions, and progress in the current browser, and creates a new local profile. It does not delete scores already sent to the public service. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

On a shared device, another person using the same browser can see saved progress. Use Reset when you want to clear it. For questions about a public score, use the contact route without posting private details.

Articles and student response boxes

Written responses and quizzes on article and student activity pages stay on the open page. They are not saved or submitted by XiXteen. Copy written work before leaving. This differs from the automatically saved learner quiz sessions.

Links and feedback

XiXteen does not add advertising or analytics trackers. External reading links and GitHub feedback have their own privacy practices. GitHub feedback is public; do not include identifiable student information.
