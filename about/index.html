XiXteen

About & contact

XiXteen helps people practise sixteen reasoning skills: clarifying claims, evaluating evidence, handling uncertainty, and explaining a judgment.

Who makes XiXteen?

XiXteen is created and maintained by Phil Stilwell. The site brings together short multiple-choice practice, longer lessons and case studies, and teaching packs with separate student and instructor materials.

Illustrations created with ImageGen are identified on the site. Invented teaching scenarios are labelled as such; they are examples for reasoning practice, not research findings.

Choose your starting point

  • For learners: one daily question per skill, focused practice, explanatory feedback, and progress saved on your device.
  • For instructors: teaching plans, student activities, model answers, PDFs, and editable text.
  • Lessons and case studies: work through an idea in more depth.

What progress labels mean

The skill map describes your answers on this site. It is not a standardized assessment of intelligence or critical-thinking ability. Difficulty levels and suggested lesson times are provisional. A handful of questions is not enough to establish mastery.

Help test a lesson by reporting confusing questions, timing, and how well students can apply the reasoning to another example.

Contact and feedback

Send a question, correction, or classroom-use request through XiXteen's GitHub feedback form. A GitHub account is required. Reports are public, so please do not include student names, private work, contact details, or device identifiers.

Include the page address or question ID and explain what needs attention. Please use the same route for requests beyond the classroom-use permission.
